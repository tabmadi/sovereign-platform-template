# Risk Register

Every ADR states the risks that its own decision accepts, and each statement is complete for its subject. No ADR can see the total. A reader learns the cookie scope in one document, the header trust in another, and the detection latency in a third. Nothing puts them together.

This document puts them together. It adds no risk and decides none again. Every row links to the decision that accepted it, and that decision stays the only place for the reasoning.

**Severity is impact against the compensating control**, not likelihood. Take a risk with a strong control and a catastrophic reach. It ranks above a risk with a weak control and a bounded reach. The first one ends badly when the control fails.

| 1 | **A product-origin XSS uses an operator session to reach the ops tier.** One session cookie is scoped to the parent host, so it reaches every `*.ops.<host>` origin | every operator tool: the cluster's control surface, its dashboards, and its data inspector | the product-origin CSP, plus per-tool authorization and AAL2 on the ops tier. | 3 | **Nothing pages.** Out-of-hours detection is the next working day for every failure class | an overnight production fault runs until morning | the split between `page` and `ticket` is already written, so nothing is rewritten when a receiver arrives | the project states an availability objective tighter than the next working day | [ADR-0502](../adr/0502-alerting-and-on-call.md) |

The fix is rotation, so the trigger is the first rotation that nobody planned | [ADR-0202](../adr/0202-secrets.md) |

## What the composition shows

**Rows 3, 6, and 7 are one risk.** Nothing pages. The regression window is a day. The failure of the alerting stack itself is invisible. Together they mean that human attention sets the platform's mean time to detect, and its floor is the next working day. [`detection-latency.md`](detection-latency.md) states this composition as a table. An availability objective is checked against it.

**Rows 1 and 2 are the security pair.** Both give up a boundary to stay simple. Both rely on controls that are strong until they fail. Both have seams with triggers that this platform can observe. Read these two rows before you decide that this template fits a project.

**Rows 8 and 13 are the two unbounded residuals of the build path.** [`build-path.md`](build-path.md) states this composition as a table. It is the merge-path partner of `detection-latency.md`. Together the two documents give the two dominant terms. Human attention sets the run-time exposure. Whether a machine can see a property sets the merge-time exposure.

**Row 5 is the only risk that the platform causes itself, and it is on purpose.** A gate that nobody can bypass can also lock the platform out. The gate stays because the alternative is not enforcement. The alternative enforces image provenance where the deploying party can go around it, per [ADR-0203](../adr/0203-policy-enforcement.md).

**A risk leaves this register in one of two ways.** Its trigger fires and the decision is made again. Or the decision is amended and the risk is no longer accepted. No risk leaves by a quiet fix, because the row belongs to a decision and not to the code.
